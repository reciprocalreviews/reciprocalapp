# Who to contact

Reciprocal Reviews is a platform that many journals and conferences use. Who can help you
depends on what your question is about.

## A question about a journal or conference

Each venue on Reciprocal Reviews is run by its own editors, and only they can answer
questions about it: its submissions, reviewing, assignments, deadlines, decisions, or what
it pays in tokens. Write to them directly.

- Every venue's page lists its administrators, with their email addresses.
- Replying to an email we send you about a venue reaches its editors.

The stewards who run the platform don't run venues and can't answer for them, so a
question about a venue sent to the stewards only delays the answer.

## A question about the platform itself

Write to the stewards at stewards@reciprocal.reviews, or see the [contact page](/contact)
for who they are. That covers your account, getting a copy of your data or having it
deleted, proposing a new venue, and how tokens work across the platform. Replying to an
email we send you that isn't about a venue reaches them too.

## A bug or an idea for a feature

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/reciprocalreviews/reciprocalapp/issues), where
others can see it, follow it, and add to it. Please don't email these to the stewards: a
report in an inbox is one nobody else can find.
