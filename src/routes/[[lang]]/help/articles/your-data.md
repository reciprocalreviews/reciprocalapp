# Exporting or deleting your data

## Getting a copy

Your profile has an export option that gives you everything we hold about you: your
profile, the venues and roles you are involved in, your submissions and assignments, and
the full history of every token that has passed through your account.

## Deleting your account

You can ask us to erase you. What that means in practice:

- Your profile, contact email, and personal details are removed.
- Records that other people depend on are kept, but are no longer connected to you. That
  includes that a submission was reviewed, and that tokens moved between accounts. A venue's
  ledger has to stay balanced even after someone leaves, or the currency stops making
  sense for everyone still using it.
- Thank-you notes you wrote are handled the same way.

Erasure cannot be undone.

## If you can't sign in

Not everyone who wants their data removed still has an account they can get into. Write to
the stewards and one of them can act on your request out of band. Expect to be asked
something that establishes the account is yours.
